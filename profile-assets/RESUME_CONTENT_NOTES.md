  May 2023. No claim of four years entirely in Python or security employment.

  were not provided. Rankings are explicitly team achievements.

  were not provided. Do not add Arduino, ESP32, OpenCV or other guessed tools.
  Its portfolio year uses the final-year/graduation year, 2023.

  production RAG evaluation platform without project evidence.
- Package download counts remain live on the website. The static resume does
  not freeze an old count or claim an unsupported performance improvement.

  expectations are not printed on it.
