# Contractor Platforms — Ranked for Bhadri's Profile

**Your profile as a platform sees it:** 4 years experience · Technical Lead title · Rust systems (ZeroCode, ZeroVPN, NullVeil) · Python/FastAPI · two published packages, one with 16K+ PyPI downloads · based in Salem, India · strong written English (the portfolio proves it).

**Two things about you that matter more than the CV:**
1. **Rust is the lever.** Rust freelance work is scarce-talent, premium-rate: blockchain/Web3 specialists bill **$100–220/hr**, and Rust systems work carries a **30–50% premium** over general development. Rust is also *the* blockchain language — your ZeroCode/ZeroVPN work is directly relevant to the highest-paying niche, which is the exact opposite of its worth in a full-stack req.
2. **16K downloads is third-party proof.** Almost no 4-year candidate has software strangers actually install. Every one of these platforms weights work history; this is the strongest single item you own.

**The honest handicap:** most premium platforms index on 5+ years. You're at 4. Your open source partly buys past that — but expect it to matter.

---

## Ranked

### 1. Gun.io — best rate-to-effort ratio
- **Rates:** $100–200/hr. Highest floor of any platform here.
- **Screening:** algorithmic screen → work history & background review → live technical interview with a senior engineer. **~100 of ~1,000 monthly applicants accepted (~10%).**
- **Why you:** it explicitly reviews *work history*, which is where you're strongest. A published package with real downloads and a sandbox with a STRIDE threat model reads very differently from four years of CRUD.
- **Against:** US-centric client base. Timezone overlap will be the friction, not skill.

### 2. Braintrust — best economics, and a real Rust fit
- **Rates:** negotiated per client, but **the platform takes 0% from freelancers** — fees are on the client side. Your negotiated rate is what you actually receive. Every other platform on this list embeds an undisclosed markup.
- **Why you:** Web3-native, and Rust *is* the Web3 systems language. This is the one place your ZeroCode/ZeroVPN work is the headline rather than a curiosity.
- **Against:** thinnest public data of the five — I couldn't find reliable freelancer-side rate figures, so treat the economics claim as structural (zero commission is documented) rather than a proven number. Crypto exposure is a real risk if you don't want it.

### 3. Toptal — best credential, worst time cost
- **Rates:** client-side $60–200+/hr, with an **undisclosed markup baked in** — you receive less than the client pays, and you don't get told how much less.
- **Screening:** 5 stages — English interview (15–20 min, ~70–80% pass) → 90-min timed coding challenge (algorithms/data structures) → **test project taking 1–3 weeks** → live technical interview. **<3% accepted. 3–8 weeks end to end.**
- **Why you:** "Toptal" is a portable credential; passing is a signal in itself.
- **Against:** the 1–3 week unpaid test project is the real price. Budget it honestly. The algorithmic challenge is also the stage most likely to catch you out — it tests LeetCode-shaped skills, not the systems depth you actually have.

### 4. Arc.dev — good rates, but the experience bar bites
- **Rates:** contract **$60–110/hr**, and Arc notes rates are "evenly distributed globally" — meaning less India discounting than most.
- **Screening:** profile screen → behavioural interview → technical interview / pair programming.
- **Against:** Arc states candidates with **5+ years** perform best in vetting. At 4 years you're under the line they publish. Worth trying, but expect it to be the reason if it goes wrong.

### 5. Turing — go last
- **Screening:** online technical screen (algorithmic + JS/TS) → technical interview.
- **The problem:** the pay data contradicts itself. Glassdoor's India figures show **₹4L–16L**; Levels.fyi shows **₹17.9L–38.6L** for the same company and country. Both can't be right, and the low band would be *worse than your local market*. Pay is set by assessment score and client match, so there's no floor you can count on.
- **Also:** their screening is JS/TS-weighted — it tests the part of your stack that isn't your edge.

## What I'd actually do

**Start Gun.io and Braintrust in parallel.** They're the two where your specific profile — Rust + verifiable open source — does the work, and neither costs you a 3-week unpaid project to find out. Run Toptal after, if you want the credential and can spare the fortnight.

**Don't lead with "full-stack" on any of them.** The whole gap analysis pointed one way: in full-stack you're one of thousands with React and Node. Lead with Rust systems engineering, and let full-stack be the thing that makes you *more* useful, not the thing you're selling.

**Expected realistic band:** $50–80/hr to start, given 4 years plus the Rust premium. That's ₹75–130 LPA equivalent at full utilisation, against ₹13–20 LPA locally. Even at 60% utilisation it's a large multiple. Rate rises fast once you have platform history.

---

## Caveats

- Rates above are what **platforms and aggregators advertise**, which skews high — they're marketing to clients, and the top of a published range is not the typical placement.
- **Contract $/hr ≠ salary.** No benefits, no paid leave, utilisation never 100%, you carry your own tax and currency risk. Halve the headline before comparing to a salary.
- Acceptance rates (<3% Toptal, ~10% Gun.io) are **self-reported by the platforms** and are partly marketing — they make the credential sound scarcer.
- The Turing pay conflict is unresolved. If you go there, get a number in writing before investing in the assessment.
- I have **not** verified whether each platform currently onboards India-based contractors on the terms above. That's the first question to ask each of them, before any screening effort.
